ABOUT KAIRO

A calmer way to make websites useful.

Kairo turns the information already sitting on your website into a helpful, always-available conversation. It gives visitors a clear next step and gives teams fewer repetitive questions.

01 / CLARITY

Answers with context

Kairo learns from your pages and connected sources instead of guessing from a generic script.

03 / MOMENTUM

Ready for the next question

See what visitors ask, improve your knowledge, and keep the assistant useful over time.
